# Session 3.5 — AI Prompt & Algorithm Tuning
**Status:** Needs Verification
**Branch:** `feature/storybook` (or separate branch)
**Depends on:** S2 complete (generation pipeline working end-to-end)
**Can run:** any time after S2 — does not depend on S3 frontend

## Problem

The current Claude output includes details that weren't in the source data — Claude fills gaps
with plausible-sounding but unverified assumptions. Example from the demo output:

> "We laid you in your bassinet and nobody could look away. Twenty minutes passed like that,
> all of us just watching you breathe..."
> "...though your dad beat me to it."
> "Your dad and I have both seen newborns before..."

Some of this IS grounded in the journal ("Her dad definitely cried", "twenty minutes" is in
the entry). But Claude also makes structural assumptions: that there are two parents present,
that it's narrated by the mother, that the "three of us" includes a specific family structure.
Single parents, same-sex couples, or non-traditional families may find this jarring or
alienating. Beyond representation concerns, Claude is also prone to:
- Adding emotional beats not mentioned in the journal entries
- Inventing reactions ("you blinked at us with a bemused expression")
- Assuming context about relationships ("your dad", "we")

The system prompt says "do not invent specifics" but Claude interprets this loosely — it
doesn't invent facts, but it does invent emotional color and relational detail.

## Goals

1. **Reduce fabrication** — output should be grounded more tightly in source data
2. **Remove demographic assumptions** — don't assume two parents, don't assume gender of
   narrator or partner, don't reference "dad/mom" unless explicitly in the journal text
3. **Improve consistency** — chapters should feel like they come from the same voice
4. **Experiment systematically** — test prompt variants and compare output quality before
   committing to a change

## Approaches to Explore

### 1. Tighten the system prompt
Current:
> "Use only the details provided — do not invent specifics."

Stronger version:
> "You may only reference people, places, and events that are explicitly named in the
> provided journal entries. Do not infer family structure, number of parents, or anyone's
> gender. Use 'we' only if the journal entries use 'we'. If the journal says 'her dad',
> you may use that — otherwise use neutral language."

### 2. Restructure the user prompt as explicit facts
Instead of passing raw journal text (which Claude expands into narrative), pass a more
structured list of discrete facts:

```
FACTS (use only these — do not add detail):
- Lily arrived home from the hospital on [date]
- She slept during the car ride and woke up when the car stopped
- She was placed in her bassinet
- She smiled for the first time after a morning feed at week 4 — described as "a real smile, not the gassy kind"
- She did it twice
- She lifted her head during tummy time and held it for 10 seconds at week 6
```

This forces Claude to work from discrete facts rather than expanding on narrative prose.

### 3. Adjust temperature / model
- Default Anthropic temperature may allow too much creative latitude
- Try `"temperature": 0.3` for more conservative, literal output
- Try `claude-haiku-4-5-20251001` vs `claude-sonnet-4-6` — Sonnet may follow instructions
  more precisely (at higher cost)
- Make temperature configurable via `anthropic.temperature=${ANTHROPIC_TEMPERATURE:0.7}`

### 4. Few-shot examples
Include 1–2 example (input → good output) pairs in the system prompt to show Claude the
level of specificity and restraint expected. This is the highest-effort approach but likely
the most effective for style consistency.

### 5. Post-processing
Strip known assumption patterns (e.g. "your dad", "your mom", "the two of us") from output
and flag them for parent review rather than removing them silently.

## Specific Issues to Address

| Issue | Severity | Approach |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Assumes two parents ("we", "your dad and I") | High — alienates single parents | System prompt + structured facts |
| Invents emotional reactions not in source | Medium — adds color but isn't grounded | Tighter prompt |
| Adds physical details not described ("you blinked") | Low — feels vivid but fabricated | Tighter prompt |
| Inconsistent voice across chapters | Medium — feels like different authors | Few-shot examples |
| "Three of us" (assumes family size) | High — same as two-parent issue | System prompt |

## Known Gap — First-Time Photos Not Passed to Claude

First-time chapter images are captured at unlock time (the `imageUrl` from the first_time
record is stored on the chapter row and shown in the book layout). But the photo itself is
**not** passed to Claude — `appendFirstTimesContext` only sends text (label + notes).

This is intentional for now (text-only prompt, per S0 decisions), but it means Claude can't
describe or reference the photo content even when one exists. If generation quality for
first-time chapters feels thin, consider passing image URLs to Claude's vision input in a
future iteration.

## Testing Protocol

For each prompt variant:
1. Delete the existing chapter row (so it can be regenerated from scratch)
2. Re-unlock the chapter
3. Generate and read the output critically
4. Note: does it stay grounded? Does it assume family structure? Is the tone right?
5. Compare against the previous variant

Test on both:
- An event-anchored chapter (single milestone, tight context)
- A period chapter (lots of source data, more room for Claude to wander)

## Notes from S3.1 (Wizard Planning) — Address in This Session

- **Milestone context in wizard chapters**: milestones from the selected period are auto-included in the Claude prompt as background context (not user-selectable in the wizard). Evaluate whether this actually improves output quality enough to justify the extra tokens, or whether it adds noise.
- **Photo count in book view**: the wizard shows all photos from selected entries. If a period has many entries all with photos, the book view could be visually overwhelming. Consider a "hero photo + secondary grid" layout as an alternative.
- **Custom date ranges**: the wizard currently uses predefined period buckets. Evaluate allowing users to define a custom start/end date so the wizard isn't limited to fixed developmental windows.
- **Inline photos**: photos currently appear at the bottom of the chapter as a gallery ("From this time" section). A future improvement is to weave photos inline between paragraphs — e.g. a photo appears near the paragraph that references its source entry. This requires correlating paragraphs to source entries (non-trivial) or having Claude emit photo placement markers. Defer to S3.5.
- **Chronological ordering of memories in the prompt**: `appendJournalByIds` and `appendFirstTimesByIds` are called as separate blocks, so all journal entries appear before all first times regardless of date. Claude sees them in two disconnected groups rather than as a unified timeline. Fix: merge journal entries and first times into a single date-sorted list in `buildWizardPrompt` / `buildPrompt`, then emit them as one interleaved "Memories from this time" section. Requires fetching `entry_date` / `occurred_date` for both types and sorting together before appending to the prompt.
