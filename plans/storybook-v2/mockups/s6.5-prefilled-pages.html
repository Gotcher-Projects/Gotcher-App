How "prefill" works (this is the reusable bit):
  • Seed-on-display: each row shows your saved text if you've edited it, otherwise it falls back to the live milestone (name + date). So a fresh page looks full, never blank.
  • Editable: reword, clear, or add rows — your edits persist as overrides.
  • ↻ Refresh from my milestones: re-seeds the rows from your current data (with a confirm, since it overwrites edits). The one extra control prefill adds over a plain auto page.
  • Source = GET /milestones + the MILESTONES names in babyData.js, threaded into pageData. Must be html2canvas-clean at 600×800 for PDF.
