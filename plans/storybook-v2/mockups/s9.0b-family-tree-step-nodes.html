Everyone appears; the lineage still reads at a glance. Below: one step-parent, then the busier two-step case so you can judge it at print size.

Two step-parents

Watch the crowding

How to read it

  • Dashed ring + muted fill = a step-relation, drawn smaller so it reads as secondary.
  • Dashed tether = the step-parent is paired with the parent they sit beside.
  • Solid nodes + lines = the bloodline: two parents and their linked grandparents.

With one step it's clean. With two, the outer nodes crowd the page edges and shrink — legible on screen, tight in a printed 6×8 book. That's the trade to weigh.

Both step-parents show, but the row now spans nearly the full page width and the step nodes press against the margins. Beyond two, it stops fitting — so if we go this route, the sane cap is the two bio parents + one step each side, with anyone further on the People page.
