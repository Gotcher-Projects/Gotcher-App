S5 — The "Firsts" chapter: hero + gallery, built from your data

The moment-hero and gallery page renderers already exist. S5 is the wiring: it turns your existing First Times into a chapter automatically — one hero page (featured photo + your own note) followed by a gallery page (the extra photos) for each First. No AI, no "generate" button — it reads straight from your First Times and updates live as you add them.

1 · How the chapter is built (no generation)

It's pure derivation. We read your First Times in date order and lay out a page sequence. Edit a First Time's photo or note and the page changes with it — nothing to re-run.

4 · The rules (my recommendations)

Live derivation, no snapshot. The chapter is built from your current First Times every time it renders, so new First Times appear automatically and edits flow through — there is no "regenerate" step.
No AI. The hero note is your own first_times.notes text. A blank note renders as a soft placeholder. (Wording help is a later, opt-in, paid per-field assist — not this session.)
Skip empty galleries. A First with no extra photos shows only its hero page — no empty 2×2 grid.
Order. By occurred_date (oldest → newest), matching the First Times list.
The one thing to decide before building: S5 wires this chapter into the guided book (GuidedBook.jsx), which is built in S6 — and S6 isn't built yet. So either (A) do S6's guided-book shell first, then S5 slots the Firsts chapter in; or (B) S5 builds the reusable derivation + a standalone "Firsts preview" now, and the guided-book home comes in S6. I lean A (build the shell first so S5 has somewhere to live), but B lets us ship + verify the Firsts wiring sooner. Your call.
