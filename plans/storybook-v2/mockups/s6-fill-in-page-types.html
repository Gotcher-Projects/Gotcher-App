S6 — Fill-in Page Types

The handful of new designed pages the guided book needs that don't exist yet. Each is a locked layout (template + renderer in the book canvas, same pattern as Letter/Birth/People) — we set the design, you drop photos & type into the slots. Shown at the real 600×800 page ratio. Photos are placeholders.

fill — you drag photos / type auto — a tag fills itself from your data
