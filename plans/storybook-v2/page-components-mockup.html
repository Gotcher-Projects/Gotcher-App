My darling Lily,

We haven't met you yet, but you are already the biggest part of our hearts. Every night we wonder what your laugh will sound like, whose nose you'll have, and all the little things that will make you, you.

We promise to be there for every first — every step, every scraped knee, every brave new thing. We can't wait to watch you become exactly who you're meant to be.

Hurry slowly, little one. We're ready.

— With all our love, Mum & Dad xx
