What this mock shows:
  • Photo-forward card — photo on top, caption below: size pairing (hero), date, optional note, then edit/delete.
  • Portrait or landscape — chosen in the crop step like Journal/Firsts. Same caption-below layout; only the photo aspect changes (3:4 vs 3:2). Week 20 above is landscape.
  • Many-per-week allowed — Week 24 has two cards (the generic-journal use case). No unique constraint.
  • Week dividers group the timeline (same pattern as the Journal's month dividers) so multiple entries read as one section, not disconnected repeats.
  • Self-contained card — the size caption lives on the card (not just the divider), so this exact component is reusable as an S4 storybook page and a social card.
  • Real sizes from pregnancySizes.js (raspberry → banana → ear of corn). In the app the emoji is the bundled Twemoji SVG, not the OS glyph.
  • Ascending order (Week 8 → 24 → …) so scrolling = watching the bump grow.
