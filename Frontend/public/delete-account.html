Delete Your Account

We're sorry to see you go. You can request deletion of your CradleHQ account and all associated data at any time.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to michael@cradlehq.app with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  2. Include the email address associated with your CradleHQ account.
  3. We will process your request within 30 days and send a confirmation email when complete.
Email us to delete your account

What gets deleted

Upon deletion, the following data will be permanently removed from our systems:

Account & login info Baby profile Feeding logs Sleep logs Diaper logs Growth records Vaccine records Appointments Journal entries First times Milestones Photos

Retention period

All personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days of your request. Anonymized, non-identifiable analytics data may be retained.
